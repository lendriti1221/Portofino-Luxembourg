Our story

A taste of Italy in the heart of Luxembourg

Portofino brings the soul of the Italian Riviera to Luxembourg City. Every dish is crafted from seasonal ingredients: handmade pasta rolled fresh each morning, wood-fired focaccia, and classic Mediterranean mains prepared with care.

Whether you're joining us for a quiet weekday lunch or a festive dinner with family, our team welcomes you with the warmth and generosity that define Italian hospitality.
